New to Ethereum? Take it one step at a time.

Set up a wallet

Choose an Ethereum-compatible wallet from its official website or app store listing. Save your recovery phrase privately. Never share it or enter it on this site.

2

Receive ETH on Ethereum Mainnet, the intended network for BARSTRO. Check the withdrawal network before sending. Keep ETH aside for network fees.

Verify and review the swap

When a contract and official swap link are confirmed, compare the full token address with this site and its block-explorer record. Open the verified swap destination.
